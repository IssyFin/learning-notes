# Painkillers

| Medication    | Other Names | Best for |
| -------- | ------- | ------- |
| Tylenol  | Paracetamol, Panadol, Acetaminophen   | Reduces brain sensitivity to pain signals |
| Aspirin | Acetylsalicylic Acid     | Reduces inflammation. Also an antithrombotic, to reduce risk of heart attack, ischemic stroke, and blood clots. Also reduces fever|
| Ibuprofen   | Advil, Midol, Motrin    | Reduces inflammation. For fever, inflammation, arthiritus, migraines, menstual pain. Long-term use can result in kidney and liver problems. |
| Aleve | Naproxen | Reduces inflammation. For pain, cramps, arthiritis, gout, fever. |
| Excedrin | -- | Contains acetaminophen, aspirin, and caffeine; headache pain reliever |
| Caffeine |--| Mild to moderate headaches; expedites absorption of other painkillers|
| Benadryl | Diphenhydramine, Diphenylhydramine | Antihistamine; reduces allergy/cold symptoms and acts as a sleep aid|
| Percocet | Oxycodone/Paracetamol | Prescription only, potentially addictive; used for severe pain. Affects pain receptors. Combination of opiod oxycodone with acetaminophen |
| Vicodin | Hydrocodone/Paracetamol | Prescription-only; used for severe pain. Affects pain receptors. May result in low blood pressure, decreased breathing rate, liver failure. |
