### The Seven Years’ War/The French and Indian War (1754–1763)

*Wars · French, English, American · [Notion](https://app.notion.com/p/35444de2a6568003a6e8f2019b48d88a)*

The first “global” war; fought all over the world. Fought between England (and their Native American Allies) and the French (and their Native American allies). Had repercussions including the formation of Canada and the movement of the Acadians (Cajuns) to Louisiana.

**Sources**

- Khan video on the 7 years war

### Mexican-American War (1846–1848)

*Wars · American, Mexican · [Notion](https://app.notion.com/p/22344de2a65680f4a149e854168aa35c)*

![Mexican-American War](https://constitutioncenter.org/images/uploads/blog/Battle_of_Churubusco.jpg)

US vs Mexico

Fight over annexation of Texas and US desire to expand westward. US provoked Mexico to “start” the war in goal for more territory. US gained its current southwest area. Further increased tensions between North and South.

**Sources**

- [Khan Video on the Mexican-American War](https://www.khanacademy.org/humanities/us-history/civil-war-era/x71a94f19:the-mexican-american-war/v/the-mexican-american-war)

### Treaty of Guadalupe-Hidalgo (1848)

*Historical Events · American, Mexican · [Notion](https://app.notion.com/p/27044de2a65680128103ca3e89251fac)*

Concluded the Mexican-American War; US to give \$15m to Mexico and Mexico to cede large swath of land of the modern-day American Southwest (California, Texas, Arizona, New Mexico Utah, etc)
