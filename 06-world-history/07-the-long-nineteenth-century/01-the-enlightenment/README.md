### The Enlightenment

*Historical Periods · [Notion](https://app.notion.com/p/35844de2a656809d9078e75c97bc4bef)*

A period of time where much of the western world began valuing logic, reasoning, and science more highly, culminating in revolutions, new methods of government, and the advancement of science.
