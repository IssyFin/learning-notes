## Part 1: Nationalism or Not?

Before you begin, remember: nationalism is a strong sense of pride, loyalty, or identity connected to one’s nation.
Read each quote below and decide which description fits best:

    Nationalism: Pride and loyalty toward one’s nation
    Other group loyalty: Pride or loyalty to another kind of group, like a team, religion, or community
    It depends: The quote could show nationalism depending on the context or speaker

Circle the category you think fits best, and be ready to explain your reasoning.
