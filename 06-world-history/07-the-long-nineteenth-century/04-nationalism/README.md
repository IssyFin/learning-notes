## Origins and Impacts of Nationalism

### Overview

- Nations and nationalism only ~200 years old; the idea that human society is divided into autonomous groups (nations)
- Nation: A group of people speaking a common language, sharing a common culture, a sense of common destiny, and a common history
- Common idea that individuals'loyalty should be focused on the nation; each nation should be able to determine its own future (self-determination)
- Nationalism: The idea that the nations should have the right to govern itself. Sometimes expressed in the belief that one's own nation is better than other nations

### Origins

- French ruler Napoleon Bonaparte extended central government of France across Europe - sense of cohesion against its enemies - sense of history and identity
  - Fighting against the French regime *also* created nationalism in the attacked parties
- Growth in literacy and print culture; easier for ideas to spread and common bonds to form, particularly among intellectuals
- Reduction in importance of other loyalties, eg Catholic and Protestant Church
- Revolutions and strengthened ideals
- Local loyalty to the lived land
