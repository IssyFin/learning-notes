## Major historical events

- **1885**: Botswana became a British protectorate, known as Bechuanaland, to prevent annexation by South Africa.
- **1966**: Botswana gained independence and established a stable democracy under its first president, Seretse Khama.
- **2015**: The discovery of one of the world's largest diamonds highlighted Botswana's significant role in the global diamond industry.
