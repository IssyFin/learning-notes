## Major historical events

- **Declaration of Independence (1917):** Finland declared independence from Russia during the upheaval of the Russian Revolution.
- **Winter War (1939-1940):** Finland defended itself against Soviet invasion, gaining international recognition for its resilience.
- **EU Membership (1995):** Finland joined the European Union, integrating further into European economic and political systems.
