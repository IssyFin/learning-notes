## Major historical events

- **1830**: Belgium gained independence from the Netherlands after the Belgian Revolution.
- **1914-1918**: Belgium was invaded by Germany during World War I, leading to significant destruction and the "Rape of Belgium."
- **1957**: Belgium became a founding member of the European Economic Community (EEC), now the European Union.
