## Major historical events

- **Arab and Persian Influence (10th Century):** Comoros became a key trading hub for Arab and Persian merchants, influencing its culture and religion.
- **French Colonization (1841):** France began its colonization of the islands, which lasted until independence.
- **Independence (1975):** Comoros gained independence from France, though political instability followed with numerous coups.
