## Major historical events

- **1. Carib Wars (1772-1796)** – The indigenous Garifuna people fiercely resisted British rule, leading to two wars and their eventual exile.
- **2. La Soufrière Eruptions (1812, 1902, 1979, 2021)** – The volcano has erupted multiple times, causing devastation and shaping the island’s history.
- **3.** **Independence (1979)** – Saint Vincent and the Grenadines gained independence from Britain on October 27, 1979.
