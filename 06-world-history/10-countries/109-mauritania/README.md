## Major historical events

- 1. **1960**: Mauritania gained independence from France.
- 2. **1978**: A **bloodless military coup** ousted President Moktar Ould Daddah.
- 3. **2005**: Another **military coup** removed President Maaouya Ould Sid'Ahmed Taya while he was attending King Fahd’s funeral in Saudi Arabia.
