## Major historical events

- **1. Independence from the Soviet Union (1991)** – Ukraine declared independence following the dissolution of the USSR.
- 2. **Orange Revolution (2004-2005)** – Mass protests led to the annulment of a fraudulent presidential election and a re-run that brought Viktor Yushchenko to power.
- 3. **Russian Invasion (2022-Present)** – Ukraine has been defending against a full-scale Russian invasion, with ongoing territorial conflicts.

## Current events

- Russia intensified its advances against Ukraine
- Ukraine submitted a ceasefire plan, but Russia responded with increased aerial attacks.
- Germany pledged new military aid, including long-ranged missiles for Ukraine
