## Major historical events

- **1. Independence from Brazil (1825-1828)** – Uruguay declared independence and was recognized after peace talks between Brazil and Argentina.
- 2. **Civil Wars (1838-1851)** – Conflicts between political factions shaped Uruguay’s political landscape.
- 3. **Military Dictatorship (1973-1985)** – A period of extreme repression, with Uruguay known as "the torture chamber of Latin America".
