## Major historical events

- **1. Dutch Colonization (1667)** – The British ceded Suriname to the Dutch in exchange for New Amsterdam (now New York City).
- 2. **Independence (1975)** – Suriname gained independence from the Netherlands, leading to mass emigration to Europe.
- 3. **December Murders (1982)** – A military coup led by Dési Bouterse resulted in the execution of 15 opposition leaders, triggering international sanctions.

## Current events

- **Suriname votes in parliamentary elections**, aiming to transform its economy with newly discovered oil reserves.
- **UNESCO-backed project digitizes Jewish history**, preserving thousands of archival documents.
- **Corentyne River bridge project moves forward**, strengthening ties between Suriname and Guyana.
