## Major historical events

- **1991**: Kyrgyzstan gained independence from the Soviet Union, marking the beginning of its journey as a sovereign nation.
- **2005**: The Tulip Revolution led to the ousting of President Askar Akayev, highlighting public dissatisfaction with corruption and governance.
- **2010**: Ethnic clashes between Kyrgyz and Uzbeks in southern Kyrgyzstan resulted in significant violence and displacement, prompting constitutional reforms to stabilize the country.

## Current events

- Economic challenges and limited industrial development. Focusing on strengthening ties with central Asian countries, with navigation of relations with Russia and China. Addressing domestic challenges, such as press freedom and minority rights.
