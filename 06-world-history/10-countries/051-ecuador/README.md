## Major historical events

- **Inca Conquest (15th Century):** Ecuador became part of the Inca Empire under Huayna Capac.
- **Independence (1822):** Ecuador gained independence from Spain after the Battle of Pichincha.
- **Oil Boom (1960s):** The discovery of oil transformed Ecuador's economy.
