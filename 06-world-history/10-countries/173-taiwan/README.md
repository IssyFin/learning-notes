## Major historical events

- **1. Retrocession Day (1945)** – Japan surrendered Taiwan to General Chiang Kai-shek, marking the end of Japanese rule.
- 2. **Taiwan Strait Crisis (1954-1955, 1958)** – A series of military conflicts between China and Taiwan, shaping cross-strait relations.
- 3. **Democratization (1987-1996)** – Taiwan transitioned from martial law to a multi-party democracy, culminating in its first direct presidential election in 1996
