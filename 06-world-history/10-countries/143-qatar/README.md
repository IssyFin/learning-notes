## Major historical events

- **1. Discovery of Oil & Gas (1939–1940s)** – Qatar’s economy transformed with the discovery of oil and natural gas, leading to rapid modernization.
- 2. **Independence from Britain (1971)** – Qatar became fully sovereign, ending its status as a British protectorate.
- 3. **FIFA World Cup 2022** – Qatar became the first Middle Eastern country to host the FIFA World Cup, showcasing its global influence.

## Current events

- Focusing on economic diversification beyond oil and gas, specifically in technology, finance, and tourism. Increasing its liquified production capacity and playing a diplomatic role in regional affairs.
