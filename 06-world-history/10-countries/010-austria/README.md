## Major historical events

- **Austro-Hungarian Empire Formation (1867)**: Austria and Hungary united under a dual monarchy.
- **Anschluss (1938)**: Austria was annexed by Nazi Germany, a controversial chapter in its history.
- **State Treaty (1955)**: Austria regained independence and declared neutrality after World War II.
