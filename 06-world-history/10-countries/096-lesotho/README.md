## Major historical events

- **1824**: Moshoeshoe I unified various clans to form the Basotho nation, establishing his stronghold at Thaba Bosiu.
- **1868**: Basutoland (now Lesotho) became a British protectorate after Moshoeshoe I appealed for protection against Boer invasions.
- **1966**: Lesotho gained independence from Britain, becoming the Kingdom of Lesotho.
