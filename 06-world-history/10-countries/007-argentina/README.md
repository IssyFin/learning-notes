## Major historical events

- **May Revolution (1810)**: This marked the beginning of Argentina's struggle for independence from Spain.
- **Declaration of Independence (1816)**: Argentina officially declared independence from Spanish rule.
- **Dirty War (1976-1983)**: A period of state terrorism during military dictatorship, with thousands of disappearances.
