## Major historical events

- 1. **Partitions of Poland (1772–1795)** – Poland was divided among Russia, Prussia, and Austria, effectively erasing it from the map for over a century.
- 2. **World War II & Nazi Occupation (1939–1945)** – Poland was invaded by Germany and the Soviet Union, suffering immense destruction and the Holocaust.
- 3. **Solidarity Movement & Fall of Communism (1980s–1989)** – The Solidarity trade union, led by **Lech Wałęsa**, played a key role in ending communist rule in Poland
