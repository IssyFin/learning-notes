## Major historical events

- **Independence**: Grenada gained independence from Britain on February 7, 1974.
- **Revolution and U.S. Invasion**: The 1979 coup by the New Jewel Movement led to a Marxist government, which was overthrown by a U.S.-led invasion in 1983.
- **Hurricane Ivan**: In 2004, Hurricane Ivan devastated the island, causing widespread damage.

## Current events

- Focusing on economic development and addressing labor shortages
