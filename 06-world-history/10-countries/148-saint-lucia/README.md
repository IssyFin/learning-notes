## Major historical events

- **1. Colonial Struggles (17th-19th Century)** – Saint Lucia changed hands between the British and French** 14 times**, earning the nickname "Helen of the West Indies".
- **2.** **Abolition of Slavery (1834)** – Slavery was abolished, leading to significant social and economic changes.
- 3**. Independence (1979)** – Saint Lucia became an independent nation on February 22, 1979, after being an associated state of the UK since 1967.
