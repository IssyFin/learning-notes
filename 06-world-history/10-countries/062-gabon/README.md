## Major historical events

- **Colonial Era**: Gabon became part of French Equatorial Africa in 1910 and gained independence from France on August 17, 1960.
- **Longest-Serving Leader**: Omar Bongo ruled Gabon from 1967 to 2009, making him one of the longest-serving heads of state in the world.
- **Recent Coup**: In August 2023, a military coup ousted President Ali Bongo Ondimba, ending his family's 50-year rule.

## Current events

- Transitional military rule, with General Brice Oligui Nguema serving as interim leader; elections planned for April 2025
