## Major historical events

- **1. Spanish Colonization (1521–1898)** – The Philippines was under Spanish rule for over **300 years**, shaping its culture and governance.
- 2. **Philippine Revolution & Independence (1896–1946)** – The revolution against Spain led to U.S. rule, with full independence granted in **1946**.
- 3. **People Power Revolution (1986)** – A peaceful uprising ousted **Ferdinand Marcos**, restoring democracy
