## Major historical events

- **The Kalmar Union (1397):** Denmark, Sweden, and Norway united under a single monarch, Queen Margrethe I, marking a significant period in Scandinavian history.
- **The Danish Constitution (1849):** Denmark transitioned from an absolute monarchy to a constitutional monarchy, establishing democratic governance.
- **World War II Occupation (1940-1945):** Denmark was occupied by Nazi Germany but maintained a resistance movement and protected many of its Jewish citizens.
