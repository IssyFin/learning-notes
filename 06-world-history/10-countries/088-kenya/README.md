## Major historical events

- **1963**: Kenya gained independence from British colonial rule, marking the end of decades of struggle, including the Mau Mau rebellion. Jomo Kenyatta became the first Prime Minister and later the first President.
- **2007-2008**: Post-election violence erupted following disputed presidential election results, leading to significant political and social unrest. This period highlighted the need for electoral and constitutional reforms.
- **2010**: Kenya adopted a new constitution, introducing significant changes such as devolved governance and enhanced civil rights protections.

## Current events

- Addressing drought and food insecurity, economic recovery post-pandemic, with initiatives for tourism, agriculture, and technology. Continuing regional security and diplomacy, and mediating conflicts (eg in Sudan and Somalia)
