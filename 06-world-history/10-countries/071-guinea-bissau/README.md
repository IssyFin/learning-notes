## Major historical events

- **Kaabu Empire**: The region was part of the Kaabu Empire, a powerful state in West Africa, until its decline in the 19th century.
- **Independence War**: Guinea-Bissau fought a long liberation war against Portuguese colonial rule, gaining independence in 1974.
- **Civil War**: A civil war erupted in 1998–1999, leading to significant political and economic instability.

## Current events

- Attempted coup in late 2023
- Challenges with governance and economic recovery
