## Major historical events

- **1. Colonial Rule and Independence (1884-1960)** – Somalia was divided among European powers, with Britain, Italy, and France controlling different regions. It gained independence in 1960, uniting British Somaliland and Italian Somaliland.
- 2. **Somali Civil War (1991-Present)** – The collapse of the central government in 1991 led to decades of conflict, with various factions vying for control.
- 3. **Piracy Crisis (2000s-2010s)** – Somali pirates disrupted international shipping, prompting global naval interventions.

## Current events

- **Flooding in Mogadishu** has killed seven people, causing widespread damage.
- **Al-Shabab attacks** continue, with recent battles over strategic military bases.
- **Somalia bans entry of Taiwan citizens** in an effort to align with China.
