## Major historical events

- **681**: The First Bulgarian Empire was established, marking the beginning of Bulgaria as a state.
- **1878**: Bulgaria gained autonomy from the Ottoman Empire following the Russo-Turkish War.
- **1946**: Bulgaria became a communist state under Soviet influence, transitioning to democracy in 1990.
