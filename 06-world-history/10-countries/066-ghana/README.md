## Major historical events

- **Independence**: Ghana became the first sub-Saharan African country to gain independence from colonial rule on March 6, 1957.
- **Pan-African Leadership**: Under Kwame Nkrumah, Ghana played a leading role in the Pan-African movement and the formation of the Organization of African Unity (OAU) in 1963.
- **Economic Reforms**: The 1983 Economic Recovery Program marked a turning point in Ghana's economic policies, leading to significant growth.

## Current events

- Combating illegal mining (galamsey) which causes environmental damage
- Addressing environmental challenges like inflation and public debt
- Overview of Pan-African Movement: Political and cultural ideology advocating for unity+solidarity of African nations and diaspora. Early advocacy in 19th century by WEB Du Bois, Pan-African congresses starting in 1900, and resulted in decolonization.
