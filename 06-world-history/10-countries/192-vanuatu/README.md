## Major historical events

- **1. European Contact (1606)** – Portuguese explorer Pedro Fernandes de Queirós was the first European to reach Vanuatu.
- 2. **Joint British-French Rule (1906-1980)** – Vanuatu was governed as the New Hebrides under a unique Anglo-French condominium.
- 3. **Independence (1980)** – Vanuatu gained independence from Britain and France, becoming a sovereign nation.
