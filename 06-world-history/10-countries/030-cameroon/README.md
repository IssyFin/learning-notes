## Major historical events

- **German Colonization (1884):** Cameroon became a German protectorate, shaping its early modern history.
- **Independence (1960):** Gained independence from France, with Ahmadou Ahidjo as its first president.
- **Anglophone Crisis (2016–present):** Ongoing conflict between the government and separatist groups in English-speaking regions.

## Current events
