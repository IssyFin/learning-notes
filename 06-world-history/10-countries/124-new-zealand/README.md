## Major historical events

- **1. Treaty of Waitangi (1840)** – This agreement between the British Crown and Māori chiefs established British sovereignty but led to disputes over land rights.
- **2. Women’s Suffrage (1893)** – New Zealand became the first self-governing country to grant women the right to vote.
- **3. Christchurch Earthquake (2011)** – A devastating earthquake struck Christchurch, causing widespread damage and loss of life.
