## Major historical events

- **First Fleet Arrival (1788)**: British settlers established a penal colony in Sydney, marking the start of European colonization.
- **Federation (1901)**: Australia became a unified nation, forming the Commonwealth of Australia
- **Port Arthur Massacre (1996)**: A mass shooting that led to some of the world's strictest gun control laws.
