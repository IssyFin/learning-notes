## Major historical events

- **1. European Contact (1722)** – Dutch explorer Jacob Roggeveen was the first European to sight Samoa, marking the beginning of foreign interactions.
- **2. German and New Zealand Rule (1899-1962)** – Samoa was divided between Germany and the U.S. in 1899, with New Zealand taking control after World War I until independence.
- **3.** **Independence (1962)** – Samoa became the first Pacific Island nation to gain independence from colonial rule.
