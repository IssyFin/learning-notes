## Major historical events

- **1991**: Kazakhstan declared independence from the Soviet Union, marking the beginning of its journey as a sovereign nation. Nursultan Nazarbayev became the first president
- **1997**: The capital was officially moved from Almaty to Astana (now called Nur-Sultan), symbolizing a new era of development and modernization.
- **2001**: Kazakhstan joined the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO), strengthening its regional ties and international presence

## Current events

- Focusing on economic diversification, away from oil and gas, and investing in renewable energy technology. Balancing relations with Russia and China while maintaining West relationships. Improving education, healthcare, and government transparency
