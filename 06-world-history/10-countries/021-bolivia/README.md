## Major historical events

- **1825**: Bolivia gained independence from Spanish rule, named after Simón Bolívar.
- **1879-1884**: The War of the Pacific resulted in Bolivia losing its coastline to Chile, making it a landlocked country.
- **1952**: The Bolivian National Revolution brought significant social and economic reforms, including land redistribution and universal suffrage.
