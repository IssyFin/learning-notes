## Major historical events

- **Independence from France (1960):** Chad gained independence, with François Tombalbaye as its first president.
- **Civil War (1965–1979):** A prolonged conflict between the government and rebel groups, exacerbated by ethnic and regional tensions.
- **Toyota War (1986–1987):** A decisive conflict between Chad and Libya, named for the use of Toyota pickup trucks in combat.

## Current events
