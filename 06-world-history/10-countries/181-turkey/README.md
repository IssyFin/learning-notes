- 2. **Establishment of the Republic (1923)** – Mustafa Kemal Atatürk founded modern Turkey, transitioning from an empire to a secular republic.
- 3. **Failed Coup Attempt (2016)** – A military faction attempted to overthrow President Erdoğan, leading to a government crackdown.
