## Major historical events

- **Maya Civilization**: The ancient Maya civilization thrived in Guatemala, leaving behind iconic sites like Tikal.
- **Independence**: Guatemala gained independence from Spain on September 15, 1821.
- **Civil War**: The Guatemalan Civil War (1960–1996) resulted in significant human rights violations, including the genocide of Indigenous Maya.
