## Major historical events

- **1795**: Belarus became part of the Russian Empire after the partitions of the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth.
- **1919**: The Byelorussian Soviet Socialist Republic was established, later becoming part of the Soviet Union.
- **1991**: Belarus declared independence following the dissolution of the Soviet Union.

## Current events

- Closely allied with Russia
- Facing allegations of human rights violations and political inconsistencies with voting
