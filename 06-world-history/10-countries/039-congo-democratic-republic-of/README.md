## Major historical events

- **Congo Free State (1885–1908):** Under King Leopold II of Belgium, the region suffered exploitation and atrocities.
- **Independence (1960):** The DRC gained independence from Belgium, leading to political turmoil.
- **First and Second Congo Wars (1996–2003):** These conflicts involved multiple nations and caused significant loss of life.

## Current events

- Ongoing conflicts in eastern regions
- Focusing on infrastructure development and international partnerships
