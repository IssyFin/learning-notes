## Major historical events

- **Battle of Avarayr (451 AD)**: Armenians fought against the Sassanid Empire to preserve their Christian faith, a pivotal moment in Armenian history.
- **Armenian Genocide (1915-1917)**: A tragic period during which the Ottoman Empire systematically killed 1.5 million Armenians.
- **Independence (1991)**: Armenia declared independence from the Soviet Union.

## Current events
