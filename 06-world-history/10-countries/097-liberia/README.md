## Major historical events

- **1847**: Liberia declared independence, becoming Africa's first republic founded by freed American slaves.
- **1980**: A coup led by Samuel Doe ended over 130 years of Americo-Liberian political dominance.
- **1990-2003**: Liberia experienced two civil wars, causing widespread devastation and displacement.
