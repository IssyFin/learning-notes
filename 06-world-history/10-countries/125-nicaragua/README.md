## Major historical events

- **1. Sandinista Revolution (1979)** – The Sandinista National Liberation Front overthrew the Somoza dictatorship, leading to major political shifts.
- **2. Iran-Contra Affair (1980s)** – The U.S. secretly funded Contra rebels fighting the Sandinista government, leading to international controversy.
- **3. Hurricane Mitch (1998)** – One of the deadliest hurricanes in Central American history, causing widespread devastation in Nicaragua.

## Current events

- Human rights concerns and political tension. Scrutiny over press freedoms, and conflict between government and opposition groups
