## Major historical events

- **Cuban Revolution (1959):** Fidel Castro's forces overthrew Batista's regime, establishing a communist state.
- **Bay of Pigs Invasion (1961):** A failed U.S.-backed attempt to overthrow Castro's government.
- **Cuban Missile Crisis (1962):** A major Cold War confrontation between the U.S. and the Soviet Union over missiles in Cuba.
