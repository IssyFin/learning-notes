## Major historical events

- **1961**: Kuwait gained independence from British rule, marking the establishment of the modern state. Sheikh Abdullah Al-Salem Al-Sabah became the Emir and is often referred to as the "Father of Independence."
- **1990-1991**: The Gulf War occurred after Iraq invaded Kuwait, leading to a coalition-led military intervention to liberate the country.
- **2005**: Women in Kuwait gained the right to vote and run for office, a significant milestone in the country's political and social development.

## Current events
