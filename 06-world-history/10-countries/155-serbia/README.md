## Major historical events

- **1. Battle of Kosovo (1389)** – A pivotal battle between Serbian forces and the Ottoman Empire, shaping Serbian national identity.
- 2. **Breakup of Yugoslavia (1990s)** – Serbia played a central role in the dissolution of Yugoslavia, leading to conflicts in the Balkans.
- 3. **Kosovo Independence Declaration (2008)** – Kosovo declared independence from Serbia, a move Serbia has not recognized.

## Current events

- **Student Protests** – Serbian students are demanding snap elections and accountability following a tragic rail station collapse.
- **Government Crackdown** – Officials have denied using sonic weapons against protesters, despite allegations.
- **Political Shifts** – Serbian Prime Minister Milos Vucevic resigned after months of mass protests.
