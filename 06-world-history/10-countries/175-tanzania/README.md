## Major historical events

- **1. Independence (1961)** – Tanganyika gained independence from Britain, later merging with Zanzibar to form Tanzania in 1964.
- 2. **Arusha Declaration (1967)** – President Julius Nyerere introduced Ujamaa socialism, shaping Tanzania’s economic policies.
- 3. **Uganda-Tanzania War (1978-1979)** – Tanzania played a key role in overthrowing Ugandan dictator Idi Amin.
