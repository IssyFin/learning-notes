## Major historical events

- **1463**: Bosnia was annexed by the Ottoman Empire, marking centuries of Ottoman rule.
- **1992-1995**: The Bosnian War followed the breakup of Yugoslavia, resulting in significant loss of life and displacement.
- **1995**: The Dayton Agreement ended the Bosnian War and established the current political structure.
