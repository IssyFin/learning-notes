## Major historical events

- **1798**: The Battle of St. George's Caye marked a decisive victory for British settlers over Spanish forces.
- **1862**: Belize was declared a British Crown Colony, known as British Honduras.
- **1981**: Belize gained full independence from the United Kingdom.
