## Major historical events

- **Khmer Empire (802–1431):** The rise and fall of one of Southeast Asia's most powerful empires, known for its architectural marvels like Angkor Wat.
- **Khmer Rouge Regime (1975–1979):** A tragic period of genocide under Pol Pot, leading to the deaths of nearly two million people.
- **Paris Peace Agreements (1991):** Marked the end of decades of conflict and paved the way for democratic elections.

## Current events

- Focusing on infrastructure projects, including (controversial) canal linking Mekong River to the Sea; neighboring countries are concerned
