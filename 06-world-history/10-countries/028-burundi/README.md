## Major historical events

- **1962**: Burundi gained independence from Belgium, becoming a sovereign nation.
- **1972**: A genocide against the Hutu population occurred during a rebellion, leaving lasting scars on the nation.
- **1993-2005**: The Burundian Civil War erupted following the assassination of the first democratically elected president, Melchior Ndadaye.
