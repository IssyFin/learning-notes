## Major historical events

- **1979**: Kiribati gained independence from the United Kingdom, becoming a sovereign nation. This marked the end of British colonial rule over the Gilbert Islands.
- **1995**: Kiribati shifted the International Date Line to ensure all its islands were in the same time zone, a move that symbolized unity and practicality for the nation.
- **2008**: Kiribati's President Anote Tong began international advocacy on climate change, highlighting the existential threat posed by rising sea levels to the island nation.

## Current events
