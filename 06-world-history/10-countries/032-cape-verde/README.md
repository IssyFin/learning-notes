## Major historical events

- **Portuguese Colonization (1462):** The first settlers arrived, establishing Ribeira Grande, the oldest European city in the tropics.
- **Independence (1975):** Gained independence from Portugal after centuries of colonial rule.
- **Joining the WTO (2008):** Became a member of the World Trade Organization, boosting its global economic ties.
