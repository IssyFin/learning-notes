## Major historical events

- 1. **1964**: Malawi gained independence from the United Kingdom.
- 2. **1994**: The country transitioned to a multi-party democracy after decades of one-party rule.
- 3. **2019**: Malawi became the first country to launch a **malaria vaccine program**, marking a major health milestone.
