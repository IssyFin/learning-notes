## Major historical events

- 1. **Colonial Rule & Australian Administration (1884–1975)** – Papua New Guinea was divided between British and German colonial rule before coming under Australian administration until independence.
- 2. **World War II Battles (1942–1945)** – Papua New Guinea was a major battleground in the Pacific, with key battles such as the **Kokoda Track Campaign**.
- 3. **Independence (1975)** – Papua New Guinea gained independence from Australia on **September 16, 1975**, with **Michael Somare** as its first Prime Minister.
