## Major historical events

- **1500**: Brazil was officially discovered by Portuguese navigator Pedro Álvares Cabral, marking the beginning of Portuguese colonization.
- **1822**: Brazil declared independence from Portugal, leading to the establishment of the Empire of Brazil.
- **1888**: Slavery was abolished in Brazil, making it the last country in the Americas to do so.
