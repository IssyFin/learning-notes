- **Independence (1821):** Gained independence from Spain as part of the Federal Republic of Central America.
- **Abolition of the Army (1949):** Costa Rica abolished its military, focusing on education and healthcare instead.
- **Environmental Leadership (1990s):** Became a global leader in ecotourism and environmental conservation.
