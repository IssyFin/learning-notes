## Major historical events

- 1. **1521**: The Spanish conquest of the Aztec Empire led to the fall of Tenochtitlán and the establishment of colonial rule.
- 2. **1810**: The Mexican War of Independence began with the **Grito de Dolores**, leading to Mexico's independence from Spain in 1821.
- 3. **1910**: The Mexican Revolution started, reshaping the country's political and social landscape.
