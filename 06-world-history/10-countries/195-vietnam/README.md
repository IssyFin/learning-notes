## Major historical events

- **1. Vietnam War (1955-1975)** – A prolonged conflict between North and South Vietnam, with U.S. involvement, ended with reunification.
- 2. **Doi Moi Economic Reforms (1986)** – Vietnam transitioned from a centrally planned economy to a market-oriented system.
- 3. **Joining ASEAN (1995)** – Vietnam became a member of the Association of Southeast Asian Nations, boosting regional cooperation.
