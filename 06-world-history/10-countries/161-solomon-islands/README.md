## Major historical events

- **1. World War II Battles (1942-1943)** – The Solomon Islands were a key battleground in the Pacific, including the famous Battle of Guadalcanal.
- 2. **Independence (1978)** – The Solomon Islands gained independence from Britain on July 7, 1978.
- 3. **Ethnic Tensions and Unrest (1998-2003)** – A period of ethnic conflict led to international intervention and peacekeeping efforts
