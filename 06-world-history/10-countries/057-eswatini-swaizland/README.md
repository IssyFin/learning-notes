## Major historical events

- **Formation of the Swazi Nation (19th Century): **King Sobhuza I united various clans to form the Swazi nation, laying the foundation for modern Eswatini.
- **Independence (1968)**: Eswatini gained independence from British colonial rule, with King Sobhuza II as its leader.
- **Name Change (2018): **King Mswati III officially changed the country's name from Swaziland to Eswatini to reflect its cultural heritage.
