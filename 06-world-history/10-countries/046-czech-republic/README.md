## Major historical events

- **Velvet Revolution (1989):** A peaceful transition from communist rule to democracy, leading to the end of Czechoslovakia's communist regime.
- **Velvet Divorce (1993):** The amicable split of Czechoslovakia into the Czech Republic and Slovakia.
- **Joining the EU (2004):** The Czech Republic became a member of the European Union, marking a significant step in its integration with Western Europe.
