## Major historical events

- **1. British Protectorate (1892)** – Tuvalu became part of the British Gilbert and Ellice Islands Colony.
- 2. **Independence (1978)** – Tuvalu separated from the Gilbert Islands and became an independent nation.
- 3. **Climate Change Advocacy (2000s-Present)** – Tuvalu has been at the forefront of global climate change discussions due to rising sea levels.

## Current events

- Faces threats with rising sea levels; international efforts to address its vulnerabilities.
- Transitioning to a cashless economy by introducing ATMs
