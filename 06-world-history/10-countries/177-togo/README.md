## Major historical events

- **1. German Colonization (1884-1914)** – Togo became a German protectorate, known as Togoland, until World War I.
- 2. **Independence (1960)** – Togo gained independence from France on April 27, 1960.
- 3. **Eyadéma’s Rule (1967-2005)** – Gnassingbé Eyadéma seized power in a coup and ruled for nearly four decades.
