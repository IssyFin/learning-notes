## Major historical events

- **1. Transfer from Denmark to the U.S. (1917)** – The U.S. purchased the islands for \$25 million, fearing German control during WWI.
- 2. **Civil Rights & Governance (20th Century)** – Virgin Islanders gained U.S. citizenship in 1927 and the right to elect their own governor in 1970.
- 3. **Hurricane Devastation (Recent Years)** – The islands have faced severe hurricanes, impacting infrastructure and economy.
