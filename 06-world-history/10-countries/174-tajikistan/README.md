## Major historical events

- **1. Soviet Rule (1929-1991)** – Tajikistan was part of the Soviet Union until gaining independence in 1991.
- 2. **Tajikistani Civil War (1992-1997)** – A devastating conflict between government forces and opposition groups, ending with a peace agreement.
- 3. **Economic and Political Reforms (2000s-Present)** – Efforts to stabilize the economy and strengthen governance following the civil war.
