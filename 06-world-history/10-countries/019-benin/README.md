## Major historical events

- **1600s-1800s**: The Kingdom of Dahomey, a powerful pre-colonial state, played a central role in the transatlantic slave trade.
- **1894**: Benin became a French colony after the Second Franco-Dahomean War.
- **1960**: Benin gained independence from France.
