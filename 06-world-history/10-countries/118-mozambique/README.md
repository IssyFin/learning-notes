## Major historical events

- **1498**: Portuguese explorer **Vasco da Gama** visited Mozambique Island, marking the beginning of European influence.
- **1975**: Mozambique gained independence from Portugal after a prolonged liberation struggle.
- **1992**: The **Rome General Peace Accords** ended a 16-year civil war, bringing stability to the country

## Current events

- Post-election violence and urban riots.
- Opposition protests and boycotts
- Mass jailbreak: \~1500 prisoners escaped
