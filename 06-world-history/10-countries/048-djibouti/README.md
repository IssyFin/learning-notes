## Major historical events

- **French Somaliland (1896):** Djibouti became a French colony, serving as a strategic port in the Horn of Africa.
- **Independence (1977):** Djibouti gained independence from France, with Hassan Gouled Aptidon becoming its first president.
- **Ethiopia-Eritrea Peace Talks (2000):** Djibouti hosted peace negotiations, showcasing its role as a neutral mediator in regional conflicts.
