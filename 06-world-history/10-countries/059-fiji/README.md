## Major historical events

- **Cession to Britain (1874):** Fiji became a British colony after ceding sovereignty to Queen Victoria, marking the start of colonial rule.
- **Independence (1970):** Fiji gained independence from Britain, transitioning to a sovereign nation.
- **Military Coups (1987):** A series of coups led by Sitiveni Rabuka reshaped Fiji's political landscape, declaring it a republic.
