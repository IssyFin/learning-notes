## Major historical events

- 1. **Battle of Peleliu (1944)** – A brutal World War II battle between U.S. and Japanese forces, resulting in heavy casualties.
- 2. **Compact of Free Association (1986)** – Palau signed an agreement with the U.S., granting financial aid and defense support in exchange for strategic cooperation.
- 3. **Independence (1994)** – Palau became fully sovereign after decades of foreign rule, joining the United Nations.
