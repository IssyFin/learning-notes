## Major historical events

- **Unification**: Germany was unified in 1871 under Otto von Bismarck's leadership.
- **World Wars**: Germany played central roles in both World Wars, with significant global impact.
- **Reunification**: East and West Germany were reunified in 1990 after the fall of the Berlin Wall in 1989.
