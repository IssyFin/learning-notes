## Major historical events

- **1494**: Christopher Columbus landed in Jamaica during his second voyage to the New World, marking the beginning of European influence.
- **1838**: Slavery was abolished in Jamaica, leading to significant social and economic changes.
- **1962**: Jamaica gained independence from British rule, becoming a sovereign nation.
