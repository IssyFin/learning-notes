## Major historical events

- **1951**: Libya gained independence from Italy, becoming the United Kingdom of Libya.
- **1969**: Muammar Gaddafi led a coup, establishing a revolutionary government and ruling for over four decades.
- **2011**: Gaddafi was overthrown during the Arab Spring, leading to ongoing political instability.
