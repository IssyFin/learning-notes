## Major historical events

- 1. **1959**: The "Hutu Revolution" led to the overthrow of the Tutsi monarchy, causing many Tutsis to flee the country.
- 2. **1994**: The **Rwandan Genocide** resulted in the deaths of an estimated 800,000 people, mostly Tutsis.
- 3. **2000**: Paul Kagame became president, ushering in economic and political reform

## Current events

- In talks with the US about receiving deported migrants. Involved in peace negotiations with the DRC to resolve regional conflicts. Severed diplomatic ties with Belgium over their roles in the DRC conflict
