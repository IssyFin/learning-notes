## Major historical events

- **1. Independence from Britain (1962)** – Uganda gained independence from British colonial rule, with Milton Obote as its first Prime Minister.
- 2. **Idi Amin’s Military Coup (1971)** – General Idi Amin seized power, leading to a brutal dictatorship that lasted until 1979.
- 3. **Civil War and Rise of Yoweri Museveni (1986)** – Museveni’s National Resistance Army took control, ending years of instability and establishing a long-lasting government.
