## Major historical events

- **Soviet Era**: Georgia was part of the Soviet Union from 1921 until its independence in 1991.
- **Rose Revolution**: In 2003, peaceful protests led to the resignation of President Eduard Shevardnadze and ushered in democratic reforms.
- **2008 War**: Georgia fought a brief war with Russia over the regions of South Ossetia and Abkhazia.

## Current events

- Georgia pursuing closer ties with NATO and the EU
- Managing tensions with Russia
- Domestically focusing on political reforms and economic development
