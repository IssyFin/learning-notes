## Major historical events

- ** 1. Portuguese Occupation (1507–1650)** – The Portuguese controlled key coastal areas of Oman, including Muscat, for over a century before being expelled by local forces.
- **2. Imamate vs. Sultanate Conflict (20th Century)** – A long-standing struggle between the interior Imamate and the coastal Sultanate led to political tensions, culminating in the **Jebel Akhdar War (1954–1959)
- 3. Modernization Under Sultan Qaboos (1970–2020)** – Sultan **Qaboos bin Said al Said** transformed Oman into a modern state, improving infrastructure, education, and international relations.

## Current events

- Focusing on economic diversification to reduce reliance on oil revenues. Expanding tourism, logistics, and renewable energy. Playing a neutral, diplomatic role in foreign affairs, mediating disputes between gulf nations.
