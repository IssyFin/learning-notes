## Major historical events

- 1. **1979**: The Federated States of Micronesia became self-governing, marking a step toward independence.
- 2. **1986**: Micronesia signed the **Compact of Free Association** with the U.S., securing sovereignty while maintaining close ties.
- 3. **1991**: Micronesia joined the **United Nations**, solidifying its international presence.
