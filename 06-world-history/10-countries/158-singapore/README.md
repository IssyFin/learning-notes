## Major historical events

- **1. British Colonial Rule (1819-1963)** – Sir Stamford Raffles established Singapore as a British trading post in 1819, leading to its development as a major port.
- 2. **Japanese Occupation (1942-1945)** – Singapore fell to Japan during World War II and was renamed Syonan-To until British forces reclaimed it.
- 3. **Independence (1965)** – Singapore separated from Malaysia on August 9, 1965, becoming a sovereign nation under Prime Minister Lee Kuan Yew.
