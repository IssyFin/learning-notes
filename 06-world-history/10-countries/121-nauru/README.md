## Major historical events

- **1. Phosphate Discovery (1900s)** – The discovery of phosphate transformed Nauru’s economy, making it one of the wealthiest nations per capita for a time.
- **2. Japanese Occupation (1942–1945)** – During **World War II**, Japan occupied Nauru, leading to forced labor and displacement of many Nauruans.
- **3. Independence (1968)** – Nauru gained independence from Australia, New Zealand, and the UK, becoming the world’s smallest republic.
