## Major historical events

- **1. Independence from Yugoslavia (1991)** – North Macedonia declared independence following the breakup of Yugoslavia.
- **2. Prespa Agreement (2018)** – A landmark agreement with Greece that resolved the long-standing dispute over the country’s name, officially changing it to **North Macedonia**.
- **3. NATO Accession (2020)** – North Macedonia became a member of **NATO**, strengthening its international alliances.
