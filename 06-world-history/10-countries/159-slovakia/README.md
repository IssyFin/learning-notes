## Major historical events

- **1. Great Moravian Empire (9th Century)** – Slovakia was part of the Great Moravian Empire, which played a crucial role in spreading Christianity in Central Europe.
- 2. **Czechoslovakia Formation (1918)** – Slovakia joined the newly formed Czechoslovakia after World War I, marking a significant shift in its political landscape.
- 3. **Slovak Independence (1993)** – Following the peaceful dissolution of Czechoslovakia, Slovakia became an independent nation on January 1, 1993.
