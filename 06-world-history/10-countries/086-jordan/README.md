## Major historical events

- **1946**: Jordan gained independence from British rule and became the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan.
- **1967**: Jordan lost control of the West Bank during the Six-Day War with Israel.
- **1994**: Jordan signed a peace treaty with Israel, becoming the second Arab country to do so.
