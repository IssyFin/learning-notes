## Major historical events

- **Colonial Era**: Guyana was colonized by the Dutch and later became a British colony in 1831.
- **Independence**: Guyana gained independence from Britain on May 26, 1966, and became a republic in 1970.
- **Jonestown Tragedy**: In 1978, the Jonestown mass suicide occurred, drawing international attention to the country.

## Current events

- Rapid economic growth due to burgeoning oil industry
- Territorial disputes with Venezuela over Essequibo region.
