## Major historical events

- **Independence (1821):** El Salvador declared independence from Spain.
- **Civil War (1980-1992):** A 12-year conflict ended with peace accords mediated by the UN.
- **Bitcoin Adoption (2021):** El Salvador became the first country to adopt Bitcoin as legal tender.
