## Major historical events

- **1521**: The Bruneian Empire reached its peak under Sultan Bolkiah, controlling much of Borneo and nearby islands.
- **1888**: Brunei became a British protectorate to prevent further territorial losses.
- **1984**: Brunei gained full independence from Britain and became a sovereign nation.
