## Major historical events

- **1. Independence from Spain (1811)** – Venezuela declared independence, later joining Gran Colombia before becoming fully sovereign.
- 2. **Oil Boom (20th Century)** – Venezuela became one of the world’s largest oil exporters, shaping its economy and politics.
- 3. **Bolivarian Revolution (1999-Present)** – Hugo Chávez introduced socialist policies that transformed Venezuela’s governance.

## Current events

- 2024 presidential elections marred by allegations of fraud and repression
- US Issued a “Do Not Travel” advisory due to risks of wrongful detention and violence
- Severe inflation and poverty, millions fleeing the country
