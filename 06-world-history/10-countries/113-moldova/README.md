## Major historical events

- **1918**: Moldova (then Bessarabia) joined Romania, shaping its national identity.
- **1992**: Moldova joined the **United Nations**, marking its integration into global diplomacy.
- **1994**: A referendum resulted in Moldova rejecting reunification with Romania, solidifying its independent path

## Current events
