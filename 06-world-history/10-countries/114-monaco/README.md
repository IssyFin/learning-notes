- **1297**: The House of Grimaldi seized control of Monaco, establishing the ruling dynasty.
- **1861**: Monaco signed a treaty with France, ceding Menton and Roquebrune in exchange for independence and financial compensation
- **1993**: Monaco became a full member of the United Nations, solidifying its international presence
