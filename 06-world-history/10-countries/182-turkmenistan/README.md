## Major historical events

- **1. Russian Annexation (1881)** – Turkmenistan was incorporated into the Russian Empire after the Battle of Gok Tepe.
- 2. **Soviet Republic (1925)** – Turkmenistan became a constituent republic of the USSR, undergoing industrialization.
- 3. **Independence (1991)** – Turkmenistan declared independence following the collapse of the Soviet Union.

## Current events

- Suppression of freedoms including restrictions on movement and expression
- Reverted to a unicameral parliament, consolidating power under ex-president Gurbanguly Berdymukhamedov.
