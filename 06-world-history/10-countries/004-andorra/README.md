## Major historical events

- **Charlemagne's Charter (788)**: Tradition holds that Charlemagne granted Andorra a charter in return for their support against the Moors.
- **Paréage Agreement (1278)**: This treaty established Andorra's unique co-principality governance, shared between the Count of Foix (later France) and the Bishop of Urgell.
- **Modern Constitution (1993)**: Andorra adopted its first constitution, transitioning into a parliamentary democracy.
