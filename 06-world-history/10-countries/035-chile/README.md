## Major historical events

- **Independence from Spain (1818):** Chile declared independence after the Battle of Maipú, led by Bernardo O'Higgins and José de San Martín.
- **Military Coup (1973):** General Augusto Pinochet overthrew President Salvador Allende, leading to years of military dictatorship.
- **Return to Democracy (1990):** Chile transitioned back to democracy, ending Pinochet's rule.
