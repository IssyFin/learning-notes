## Major historical events

- **1999**: The Kosovo War ended after a NATO-led military intervention forced Serbian forces to withdraw. This marked a turning point in Kosovo's struggle for independence.
- **2008**: Kosovo declared independence from Serbia on February 17, becoming the youngest country in Europe. Over 100 countries have since recognized its sovereignty.
- **2010**: The International Court of Justice ruled that Kosovo's declaration of independence did not violate international law, solidifying its status on the global stage.

## Current events

- Strengthening international recognition and joining organizations like the UN and NATO. Improving infrastructure and addressing political instability. Tensions with Serbia, and working on joining the EU
