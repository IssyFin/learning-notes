## Major historical events

- **1. French and British Colonial Rule (1756-1814)** – Seychelles was first claimed by France in 1756, but control shifted to Britain in 1814 after the Treaty of Paris.
- 2. **Independence (1976)** – Seychelles gained independence from Britain on June 29, 1976, becoming a republic within the Commonwealth.
- 3. **One-Party Rule and Democratic Transition (1977-1993)** – A coup in 1977 led to a socialist one-party state under President France-Albert René, which lasted until multi-party democracy was restored in 1993.

## Current events

- **Cyclone Chido and Vanuatu Earthquake** – Seychelles sent condolences following recent natural disasters.
- **Blue Economy Initiatives** – The government is pushing conservation efforts and marine biodiversity protection.
- **Tourism Developments** – Air Seychelles is reestablishing direct flights to Abu Dhabi, boosting connectivity.
