## Major historical events

- **1. European Colonization (1623-1626)** – Saint Kitts was the first Caribbean island to be colonized by both the English and French, leading to conflicts and the eventual massacre of the indigenous Kalinago people.
- 2. **Sugar Plantation Economy (1650s-1900s)** – The islands became heavily reliant on sugar production, which shaped their economy and led to the importation of enslaved Africans.
- 3. **Independence (1983)** – Saint Kitts and Nevis gained independence from Britain on September 19, 1983, becoming the smallest sovereign state in the Western Hemisphere.
