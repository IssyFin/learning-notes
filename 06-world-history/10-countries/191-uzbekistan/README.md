## Major historical events

- **1. Silk Road Trade Hub (Ancient Times)** – Uzbekistan was a key center for trade and culture along the Silk Road.
- 2. **Soviet Rule (1924-1991)** – Uzbekistan was part of the Soviet Union until gaining independence in 1991.
- 3. **Andijan Massacre (2005)** – A violent crackdown on protesters led to international condemnation.
