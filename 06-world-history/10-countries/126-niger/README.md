## Major historical events

- **1. French Colonial Rule (1890s–1960)** – Niger was colonized by France and gained independence in 1960.
- **2. Tuareg Rebellions (1990s–2000s)** – Armed conflicts between the government and Tuareg groups seeking greater autonomy.
- **3. 2023 Military Coup** – A military takeover ousted President Mohamed Bazoum, leading to regional instability.

## Current events

- Political uncertainty following the 2023 coup. Economic and security challenges in the Sahel region.
