## Major historical events

- 1. **1235**: The Mali Empire was founded by **Sundiata Keita**, becoming a major West African power.
- 2. **1324**: Emperor **Mansa Musa** made his famous pilgrimage to Mecca, showcasing Mali’s immense wealth.
- 3. **1960**: Mali gained independence from France, marking the start of its modern statehood.
