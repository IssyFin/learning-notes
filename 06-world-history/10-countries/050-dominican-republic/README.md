## Major historical events

- **Independence from Haiti (1844):** The Dominican Republic declared independence, establishing itself as a sovereign nation.
- **Trujillo Era (1930-1961):** Rafael Trujillo's dictatorship left a complex legacy of modernization and human rights abuses.
- **Civil War and U.S. Intervention (1965):** A political crisis led to U.S. military involvement, shaping the nation's modern political landscape.

## Current events

- Migration challenges with Haiti. They share the island of Hispaniola, but different economies/infrastructure mean lots of migration from Haiti to the Dominican Republic - better economy. There are now stricter border controls, citizenship rules, and immigration laws, which are all controversial internationally. There’s lots of discrimination towards Haitians as a result.
