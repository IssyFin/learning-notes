## Major historical events

- 1. **Spanish Conquest & Fall of the Inca Empire (1532–1533)** – The Spanish, led by** Francisco Pizarro**, defeated the Inca Empire, marking the beginning of colonial rule.
- 2. **Peruvian Independence (1821)** – **José de San Martín** declared Peru’s independence, later solidified by **Simón Bolívar**.
- 3. **Shining Path Insurgency (1980s–1990s)** – A violent Maoist guerrilla movement led to widespread conflict and government crackdowns
