## Major historical events

- **Aksumite Empire (c. 100–940 CE):** A powerful ancient kingdom known for its trade and the adoption of Christianity.
- **Battle of Adwa (1896):** Ethiopia defeated Italy, maintaining its independence during the Scramble for Africa.
- **Ethiopian Civil War (1974–1991):** A conflict that led to the fall of Emperor Haile Selassie and the rise of the Derg regime.

## Current events

- Recovering from the Tigray conflict (a civil war in Ethiopia starting in November 2020 between federal govt and Tigray People’s Liberation Front. Caused by political tension (postponed elections and power struggles) - lasted til 2022, many lives lost, infrastructure damaged, and millions displaced)
- Efforts to rebuild infrastructure and address humanitarian needs
- Political reforms and ethnic tensions remain key challenges
