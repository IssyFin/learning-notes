## Major historical events

- **1. Independence from Britain (1964)** – Zambia gained independence, with Kenneth Kaunda as its first president.
- 2. **Economic Struggles (1990s-Present)** – Zambia has faced challenges related to debt, inflation, and political instability.
- 3. **Mining Industry Growth (2000s-Present)** – Copper mining has remained a key driver of Zambia’s economy.

## Current events

- Strengthened international relations with Poland
- Concerns over the refugee policy and cybersecurity laws
