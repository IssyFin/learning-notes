## Major historical events

- **1. Unification of Nepal (1768–1775)** – King **Prithvi Narayan Shah** unified Nepal, forming the modern nation-state.
- **2. End of the Rana Regime (1951)** – The autocratic **Rana dynasty** was overthrown, restoring power to the monarchy and paving the way for democracy.
- **3. Abolition of Monarchy (2008)** – Nepal transitioned into a **federal democratic republic**, ending centuries of monarchical rule.
