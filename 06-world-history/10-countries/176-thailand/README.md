## Major historical events

- **1. Sukhothai Kingdom (1238-1438)** – The first Thai kingdom, marking the beginning of Thai civilization.
- 2. **Ayutthaya Kingdom (1351-1767)** – A powerful kingdom that dominated Southeast Asia before being sacked by the Burmese.
- 3. **1932 Revolution** – A coup ended absolute monarchy, establishing a constitutional system.

## Current events

- Border tensions with Myanmar (Burma) - Thailand deployed fighter jets against the Myanmar military aircraft which dropped bombs near the border.
