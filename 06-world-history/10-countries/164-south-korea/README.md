## Major historical events

- **1. Korean War (1950-1953)** – The war between North and South Korea ended in an armistice, leaving the peninsula divided.
- 2. **Rapid Economic Growth (1960s-1990s)** – South Korea transformed into a global economic powerhouse, driven by industrialization and technological advancements.
- 3. **Democratization (1987-Present)** – South Korea transitioned from military rule to a democratic system, with direct presidential elections.
