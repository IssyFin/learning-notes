## Major historical events

- **
- 1. Unification of Saudi Arabia (1932)** – King Abdulaziz Ibn Saud unified the various regions of the Arabian Peninsula, establishing the modern Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.
- 2. **Discovery of Oil (1938)** – The discovery of vast oil reserves transformed Saudi Arabia into a global economic powerhouse.
- 3. **Vision 2030 (2016-Present)** – Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman launched an ambitious plan to diversify the economy and modernize the country.

## Current events

- **Trump’s Visit to Saudi Arabia** – Former U.S. President Donald Trump arrived in Riyadh for diplomatic talks and investment discussions.
- **Migrant Worker Concerns** – Human Rights Watch reported preventable deaths among migrant workers involved in Saudi infrastructure projects.
- **Defense Deals** – The U.S. and Saudi Arabia signed a historic \$142 billion defense agreement, strengthening military cooperation.
