## Major historical events

- 1. **Founding of North Korea (1948)**
- After WWII, Korea was divided at the 38th parallel. North Korea was established as the **Democratic People’s Republic of Korea** under **Kim Il-sung**.
- 2. **Korean War (1950–1953)**
- North Korea invaded South Korea, triggering a brutal war. Ended in an **armistice**, not a peace treaty—technically, the two Koreas are still at war.
- 3. **First Nuclear Test (2006)**
- North Korea conducted its first successful nuclear test, marking its entry into the nuclear club. This led to **UN sanctions** and global condemnation.

## Current events

- Actively pursuing military modernization, specifically naval expansion using missile-capable submarines.
- Allying with Russia to assist in the Ukraine conflict.
- Continues missile tests, including short-range launches and simulated nuclear strikes.
