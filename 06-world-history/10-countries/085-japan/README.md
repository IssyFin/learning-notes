## Major historical events

- **1853**: Commodore Matthew Perry's arrival ended Japan's isolationist policy, opening the country to international trade.
- **1945**: Japan surrendered in World War II after atomic bombings in Hiroshima and Nagasaki, leading to post-war reconstruction.
- **1989**: Emperor Hirohito passed away, marking the end of the Showa era and the beginning of the Heisei era.
