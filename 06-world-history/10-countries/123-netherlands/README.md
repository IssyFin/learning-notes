## Major historical events

- **1.** **Dutch Revolt (1568–1648)** – The Netherlands fought for independence from Spanish rule, leading to the formation of the Dutch Republic.
- **2.** **Golden Age (17th Century)** – The Netherlands became a global maritime power, dominating trade, finance, and the arts.
- **3.** **World War II Occupation (1940–1945)** – The country was occupied by Nazi Germany, leading to significant resistance movements and suffering.
