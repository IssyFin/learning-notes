## Major historical events

- **Italian Colonization (1890):** Eritrea became an Italian colony.
- **War of Independence (1961-1991):** Eritrea fought for independence from Ethiopia.
- **Peace Agreement (2018):** Eritrea and Ethiopia ended decades of conflict.

## Current events

- International scrutiny over human rights issues and its role in the Tigray conflict (a civil war in Ethiopia starting in November 2020 between federal govt and Tigray People’s Liberation Front. Caused by political tension (postponed elections and power struggles) - lasted til 2022, many lives lost, infrastructure damaged, and millions displaced)
