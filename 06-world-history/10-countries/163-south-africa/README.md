## Major historical events

- **1. Apartheid Era (1948-1994)** – South Africa implemented racial segregation policies, leading to international sanctions and resistance movements.
- 2. **Nelson Mandela’s Presidency (1994-1999)** – Mandela became the first Black president, marking the end of apartheid and the beginning of democratic governance.
- 3. **Economic and Social Challenges (2000s-Present)** – South Africa has faced ongoing issues such as corruption, unemployment, and inequality.
