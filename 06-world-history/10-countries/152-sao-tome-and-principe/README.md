## Major historical events

- **1. Portuguese Colonization (1470s)** – The islands were uninhabited until Portuguese explorers established settlements.
- **2. Sugar and Slave Trade (16th Century)** – São Tomé became a major hub for sugar production and the transatlantic slave trade.
- **3. Independence (1975)** – After years of colonial rule, São Tomé and Príncipe gained independence from Portugal.

## Current events

- **Political instability** continues, with legal challenges following the dismissal of the Prime Minister.
- **Investigations into human rights violations** are underway after an attempted coup.
- **Economic concerns** include efforts to revive cocoa production.
