## Major historical events

- **1. Carthaginian Empire (814 BC-146 BC)** – Carthage was a major Mediterranean power before being conquered by Rome.
- 2. **French Protectorate (1881-1956)** – Tunisia was under French control until gaining independence in 1956.
- 3. **Arab Spring (2011)** – Tunisia’s revolution led to the ousting of President Ben Ali and sparked uprisings across the region.
