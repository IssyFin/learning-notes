## Major historical events

- **Birthplace of Democracy**: Ancient Athens established the first known democracy around 508 BCE.
- **Greek War of Independence**: Greece gained independence from the Ottoman Empire in 1821 after nearly 400 years of occupation.
- **World War II Resistance**: Greece played a significant role in resisting Axis forces, notably during the Battle of Crete in 1941.
