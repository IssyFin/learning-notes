## Major historical events

- **Colonization by the British (1632)**: Antigua was settled by the British, becoming a key sugar-producing colony.
- **Abolition of Slavery (1834)**: The emancipation of enslaved people marked a significant turning point in the islands' history.
- **Independence (1981)**: Antigua and Barbuda gained independence from the United Kingdom.

## Current events

- Investing in infrastructure, including airport
