## Major historical events

- **1896**: Burkina Faso, then known as Upper Volta, became a French protectorate after the defeat of the Mossi Kingdoms.
- **1960**: Gained independence from France and became the Republic of Upper Volta.
- **1983**: Thomas Sankara came to power through a coup, initiating significant social and economic reforms before his assassination in 1987.

## Current events

- Significant unrest and security concerns; civilian casualties linked to Al Qaeda and ISIL
- Focusing on economic investment to meet food sovereignty
