## Major historical events

- 1. **1944**: The U.S. captured the Marshall Islands from Japan during World War II.
- 2. **1952**: The first thermonuclear bomb, "Ivy Mike," was detonated in the Marshall Islands.
- 3. **1986**: The Marshall Islands signed the Compact of Free Association with the U.S., gaining sovereignty while maintaining close ties.

## Current events

- Formally signed Treaty of Rarotonga to further commit to nuclear-free policies.
- Climate issues regarding rising sea levels, and environmental concerns with highly invasive frog species
