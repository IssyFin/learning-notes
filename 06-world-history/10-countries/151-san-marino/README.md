## Major historical events

- **1. Foundation (301 AD)** – According to tradition, Saint Marinus founded San Marino as a refuge from persecution, making it the world’s oldest republic.
- **2.** **Recognition of Independence (1631)** – The Papacy officially recognized San Marino’s sovereignty.
- **3. World War II Neutrality (1939-1945)** – Despite its neutrality, San Marino was briefly occupied by German forces and later liberated by the Allies

## Current events
