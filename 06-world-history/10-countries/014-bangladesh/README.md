## Major historical events

- **1947**: Partition of British India led to the creation of East Pakistan (now Bangladesh).
- **1971**: Bangladesh gained independence after a nine-month Liberation War.
- **1975**: Sheikh Mujibur Rahman, the first president, was assassinated in a military coup
