## Major historical events

- **1. Mahdist Revolt (1881-1898)** – A religious uprising led by Muhammad Ahmad, the self-proclaimed Mahdi, resulted in the overthrow of Ottoman-Egyptian rule and the establishment of the Mahdist state.
- 2. **Independence (1956)** – Sudan gained independence from British-Egyptian rule on January 1, 1956.
- 3. **Darfur Conflict (2003-Present)** – A devastating war erupted in Darfur, leading to accusations of genocide and international intervention.

## Current events

- **Drone attacks escalate Sudan’s civil war**, showcasing the rise of advanced drone warfare.
- **Sudan cuts diplomatic ties with UAE**, accusing it of supporting paramilitary forces.
- **Humanitarian crisis worsens**, with reports of civilians eating charcoal and leaves to survive.
