## Major historical events

- **1920**: France established Greater Lebanon under a League of Nations mandate, shaping its modern borders.
- **1943**: Lebanon achieved independence from French rule, becoming a sovereign state.
- **1975-1990**: The Lebanese Civil War caused widespread destruction and political instability.
