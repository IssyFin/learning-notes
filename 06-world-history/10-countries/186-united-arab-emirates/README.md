## Major historical events

- **1. Formation of the UAE (1971)** – The seven emirates united to form the UAE, marking a significant shift in governance.
- 2. **Discovery of Oil (1950s-1960s)** – Oil exports transformed the UAE into a global economic powerhouse.
- 3. **Rapid Urbanization and Development (1990s-Present)** – The UAE became known for its ambitious infrastructure projects, including the Burj Khalifa and Palm Jumeirah.
