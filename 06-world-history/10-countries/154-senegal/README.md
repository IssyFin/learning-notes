## Major historical events

- **1. French Colonization (1659-1960)** – Senegal was a key French colony, with Saint-Louis serving as an important trading post.
- 2. **Independence (1960)** – Senegal gained independence from France, with Léopold Sédar Senghor becoming its first president.
- 3. **Casamance Conflict (1982-Present)** – A separatist movement in the Casamance region has led to ongoing tensions and intermittent violence.

## Current events

- **Senegal’s World Cup Qualifiers** – The national football team recently played against Sudan in the 2026 World Cup qualifiers.
- **Art and Culture** – Senegalese curator Koyo Kouoh passed away, leaving a lasting impact on African art.
- **Political Developments** – Senegal signed a peace deal with separatist rebels, aiming to resolve one of Africa’s longest-running conflicts.
