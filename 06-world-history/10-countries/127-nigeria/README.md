## Major historical events

- **1. Independence from Britain (1960)** – Nigeria gained independence on **October 1, 1960**, marking the beginning of self-rule**
- 2. Biafran Civil War (1967–1970)** – A devastating conflict between the Nigerian government and the secessionist state of Biafra, resulting in significant casualties and humanitarian crises.
- **3. End of Military Rule (1999)** – Nigeria transitioned to democracy after decades of military rule, with **Olusegun Obasanjo** becoming the first civilian president in the Fourth Republic
