## Major historical events

- **Democratic Republic Formation (1918)**: Azerbaijan became the first secular democratic republic in the Muslim world.
- **Soviet Incorporation (1920)**: Azerbaijan was absorbed into the Soviet Union.
- **Nagorno-Karabakh Conflict (1988-Present)**: A long-standing territorial dispute with Armenia.
