## Major historical events

- **1. Colonial Rule (1505-1948)** – Sri Lanka was colonized by the Portuguese, Dutch, and British before gaining independence in 1948.
- 2. **Civil War (1983-2009)** – A brutal conflict between the government and the Tamil Tigers ended in 2009, with tens of thousands killed.
- 3. **Economic Crisis (2022-Present)** – Sri Lanka faced severe financial turmoil, leading to protests and political upheaval.
