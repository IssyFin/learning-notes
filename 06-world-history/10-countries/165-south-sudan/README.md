## Major historical events

- **1. First and Second Sudanese Civil Wars (1955-2005)** – South Sudan was embroiled in two lengthy civil wars against Sudan, fighting for autonomy and later independence.
- 2. **Independence (2011)** – South Sudan became the world's newest country on July 9, 2011, following a referendum in which nearly 99% of voters chose independence.
- 3. **South Sudanese Civil War (2013-2018)** – A political dispute between President Salva Kiir and Vice President Riek Machar led to a brutal civil war, causing immense humanitarian crises.

## Current events

- **Hospital and market bombing** in Jonglei state killed seven people, raising fears of renewed civil war.
- **Political tensions** continue as Vice President Riek Machar’s arrest threatens the fragile peace deal.
- **Flooding in Mogadishu** has caused significant damage and casualties.
