## Major historical events

- **1. Herero and Namaqua Genocide (1904–1908)** – German colonial forces carried out a brutal campaign against the Herero and Nama people, leading to mass killings and forced displacement.
- **2. Namibia’s Independence (1990)** – After decades of South African rule, Namibia gained independence on **March 21, 1990**, with **Sam Nujoma** as its first president.
- **3. SWAPO’s Armed Struggle (1966–1989)** – The South West Africa People’s Organization (SWAPO) led an armed resistance against South African control, culminating in Namibia’s independence.
