## Major historical events

- **1. Spanish-American War & U.S. Annexation (1898)** – Spain ceded Puerto Rico to the U.S. after the war, marking a shift in governance.
- 2. **Jones Act (1917)** – Puerto Ricans were granted U.S. citizenship, but the island remained an unincorporated territory.
- 3. **Hurricane Maria (2017)** – A devastating hurricane caused widespread destruction, leading to long-term recovery efforts.

## Current events
