## Major historical events

- **1918**: Latvia declared independence from Russia, establishing itself as a sovereign nation.
- **1940**: Latvia was annexed by the Soviet Union during World War II, losing its independence.
- **1991**: Latvia regained independence following the collapse of the Soviet Union.
