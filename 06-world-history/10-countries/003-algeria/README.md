## Major historical events

- **French Colonization (1830-1962)**: Algeria was a French colony for over a century.
- **War of Independence (1954-1962)**: A brutal conflict led to Algeria’s independence.
- **Civil War (1991-2002)**: A violent period marked by clashes between the government and Islamist groups.

## Current events

- President Abdelmadjid Tebboune was re-elected with a significant majority
- Economic reforms and youth unemployment
