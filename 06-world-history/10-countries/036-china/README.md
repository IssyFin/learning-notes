## Major historical events

- **Unification under the Qin Dynasty (221 BC):** The first emperor, Qin Shi Huang, unified China and began constructing the Great Wall.
- **Cultural Revolution (1966–1976):** A tumultuous period initiated by Mao Zedong to enforce communist ideology, leading to widespread upheaval.
- **Economic Reforms (1978):** Deng Xiaoping introduced market-oriented reforms, transforming China's economy.
