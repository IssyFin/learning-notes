## Major historical events

- **Independence:** Gambia gained independence from the United Kingdom on February 18, 1965.
- **Yahya Jammeh's Rule:** Jammeh ruled from 1994 to 2017 after a military coup, marked by allegations of human rights abuses.
- **2016 Transition:** Adama Barrow defeated Jammeh in the 2016 elections, leading to a peaceful transition after international intervention.
