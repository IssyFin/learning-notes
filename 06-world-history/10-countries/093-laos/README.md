## Major historical events

- **1353**: The Kingdom of Lan Xang was established by Fa Ngum, marking the beginning of Laos as a unified state.
- **1954**: Laos gained independence from France following the Geneva Accords, ending colonial rule.
- **1975**: The communist Pathet Lao took control, leading to the establishment of the Lao People's Democratic Republic.

## Current events

- Focusing on economic development, specifically with regards to hydropower and trade. However, still struggling with poverty, environmental, and infrastructure challenges.
