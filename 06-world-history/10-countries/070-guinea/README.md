## Major historical events

- **Samory Touré's Resistance**: In the late 19th century, Samory Touré led a strong resistance against French colonial forces but was eventually captured in 1898.
- **Independence**: Guinea became the first French colony in Africa to gain independence on October 2, 1958, under Ahmed Sékou Touré.
- **Military Coup**: In 2021, a military coup ousted President Alpha Condé, marking a significant political shift.

## Current events

- Under military rule, ongoing political tensions and protests against the junta (form of government led by a committee or group, typically military officials, that has seized power, often through a coup d’etat)
- Government working on transition plan to restore civilian governance.
