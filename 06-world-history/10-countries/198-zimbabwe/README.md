## Major historical events

- **1. Independence from Britain (1980)** – Zimbabwe became independent, with Robert Mugabe as its first leader.
- 2. **Land Reform Policies (2000s)** – Controversial land seizures led to economic decline and international sanctions.
- 3. **Political Shifts (2017-Present)** – Mugabe was ousted in a military coup, and Emmerson Mnangagwa took power.

## Current events

- Some economic challenges with Zimbabwe’s cement importer, due to surtax policies
