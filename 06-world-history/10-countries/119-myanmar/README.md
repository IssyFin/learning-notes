## Major historical events

- 1. **1948**: Myanmar (then Burma) gained independence from British rule.
- 2. **1962**: A military coup led by **General Ne Win** established a long-lasting authoritarian regime.
- 3. **2021**: The military seized power in a coup, sparking widespread protests and armed resistance

## Current events

- Recovering from a devastating earthquake, during the recovery of which, the military broke a ceasefire and carried out 120 attacks.
- Forced conscription program and ongoing civil war
