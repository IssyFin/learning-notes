## Major historical events

- **1. Founding of Freetown (1787)** – British abolitionists established Freetown as a settlement for freed slaves.
- 2. **Independence (1961)** – Sierra Leone gained independence from Britain on April 27, 1961.
- 3. **Civil War (1991-2002)** – A brutal civil war erupted due to political instability and diamond-fueled conflicts, ending in 2002 with international intervention.

## Current events

- **Mpox Outbreak** – Sierra Leone is experiencing a surge in mpox cases, prompting urgent public health responses.
- **Corruption Allegations** – Reports suggest Sierra Leone’s first lady and her relatives have acquired luxury properties in Gambia under questionable circumstances.
- **Commonwealth War Graves Memorial** – A new memorial is being designed to honor Sierra Leone’s WWI Carrier Corps.
