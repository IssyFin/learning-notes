## Major historical events

- 1. **Partition of India (1947)** – Pakistan was created as an independent nation following the partition of British India, leading to mass migrations and communal violence.
- 2. **Indo-Pakistani Wars (1947, 1965, 1971, 1999)** – Pakistan has fought multiple wars with India, primarily over Kashmir, with significant geopolitical consequences.
- 3. **Nuclear Tests (1998)** – Pakistan conducted nuclear tests in response to India's, becoming the world's seventh nuclear-armed state

## Current events

- Economic concerns with inflation and debt. Political tensions following recent elections and security concerns with regards to terrorism and regional instability
