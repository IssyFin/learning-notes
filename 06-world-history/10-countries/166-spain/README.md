## Major historical events

- **1. Reconquista (711-1492)** – Christian kingdoms gradually reclaimed Spain from Muslim rule, culminating in the fall of Granada in 1492.
- 2. **Spanish Civil War (1936-1939)** – A conflict between Republicans and Nationalists led to General Francisco Franco’s dictatorship.
- 3. **Democratic Transition (1975-Present)** – Following Franco’s death in 1975, Spain transitioned to democracy, joining the EU in 1986.

## Current events

- **Spain recovering from its largest blackout** in history, which disrupted transportation and daily life.
- **Copper thefts** have halted train services between Madrid and Andalusia.
