## Major historical events

- **Independence from Spain (1819):** Simón Bolívar led the liberation forces to victory at the Battle of Boyacá.
- **Thousand Days' War (1899–1902):** A devastating civil conflict between liberals and conservatives.
- **Peace Agreement with FARC (2016):** Ended over five decades of conflict with the Revolutionary Armed Forces of Colombia.

## Current events
