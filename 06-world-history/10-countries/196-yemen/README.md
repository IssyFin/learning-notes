## Major historical events

- **1. Unification of Yemen (1990)** – North and South Yemen merged to form the Republic of Yemen, but tensions persisted.
- 2. **Arab Spring Protests (2011)** – Mass demonstrations led to the resignation of President Ali Abdullah Saleh, but instability continued.
- 3. **Ongoing Civil War (2014-Present)** – A conflict between Houthi rebels and the Yemeni government has resulted in a severe humanitarian crisis.

## Current events

- At risk of returning to full-scale war, with clashes continuing in Hudaydah and Ta’iz
- Houthi attacks on vessels in the Red Sea prompt US and UK military responses
- 18+ million people remain dependent on aid
