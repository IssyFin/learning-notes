## Major historical events

- 1. **Construction of the Panama Canal (1904–1914)** – The canal revolutionized global trade, connecting the Atlantic and Pacific Oceans.
- 2. **U.S. Invasion of Panama (1989)** – The U.S. ousted dictator Manuel Noriega, citing drug trafficking and human rights violations.
- 3. **Transfer of Canal Control (1999)** – Panama gained full control of the canal from the U.S., marking a significant moment in its sovereignty.

## Current events
