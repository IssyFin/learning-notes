### The Yalta Conference (1945)

*Historical Events · American, German, Soviet, English, French · [Notion](https://app.notion.com/p/24144de2a65680c994f7e499a1d627bb)*

***US, Soviet Union, UK, and France agreed to split Germany into four zones of occupation following WWII.

***The regions worked together to win WWII, but lots of tension. Soviets signed Non-Aggression Pact with Nazi Germany in 1939, but entered the war when Hitler double-crossed Stalin and invaded Russia in 1941. ***

***US ambassador (Kennan) in Moscow warned that Soviets wanted to expand throughout the world; prescribed “containment” of communism as chief US foreign policy strategy.

Soviets nervous of US new atomic bomb. Stalled on its promises to remove troops from oil-rich regions in the Middle East and to allow free elections in conquered Poland
