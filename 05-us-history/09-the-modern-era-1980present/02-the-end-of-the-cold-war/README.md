### George HW Bush (1989–1993)

*Historical Figures · American · [Notion](https://app.notion.com/p/23a44de2a656806d817fd4dd24daad54)*

![George HW Bush](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/ee/George_H._W._Bush_presidential_portrait_%28cropped%29.jpg/250px-George_H._W._Bush_presidential_portrait_%28cropped%29.jpg)

US President (Republican)

Key role in reunification of Germany ()

Presided over invasion of panama and the Gulf War; ended Iraqi occupation of Kuwait

Negotiated and signed the North American Free Trade Agreement (US, Canada, and Mexico)

Reneged on 1988 campaign promise by raising taxes to reduce the budget deficit

Championed Americans with Disabilities Act, Immigration Act, and Clean Air Act Amendments.

Following economic recession, turnaround on tax promise, and decreased emphasis of foreign policy, lost re-election to Bill Clinton

Fun Facts:  
Second father-son pair to serve as the nation’s present (following John and John Quincy Adams)  
Second son, Jeb Bush, also sought Republican presidential nomination in 2016 primaries
