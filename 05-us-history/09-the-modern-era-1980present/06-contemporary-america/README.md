### Barack Obama (2009–2017)

*Historical Figures · American · [Notion](https://app.notion.com/p/23e44de2a656805ba941f6736828cacb)*

![Barack Obama](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/8d/President_Barack_Obama.jpg/500px-President_Barack_Obama.jpg)

US President (Democrat)

Awarded 2009 Nobel Peace Prize for efforts in international diplomacy

First term responded to the 2008 financial crisis. American Recovery and Reinvestment Act of 2009: major stimulus package to guide the economy, partial extension of Bush tax cuts, legislation to reform healthcare, and Dodd-Frank Wall Street Reform and Consumer Protection Act.

Appointed Supreme Court justices Sonia Sotomayor and Elena Kagen

Oversaw the end of the Iraq War and ordered Operation Neptune Spear (raid that killed Osama Bin Laden)

Downplayed Bush’s counterinsurgency model, expanded air strikes, and made extensive use of special forces. Ordered 2011 military intervention in Libya to implement UN Security Council Resolution of 1973, contributing to the overthrow of Muammar Gaddafi

Advocated for gun control following Sandy Hook Elementary School Shooting, signed Paris Agreement (major international climate agreement) and an executive order to limit carbon emissions. Presided over the ACA.

Initiated sanctions against Russia following the invasion in Ukraine and again after Russian interference in the 2016 US elections

Normalized relations with Cuba

Fun facts:  
First US President to publicly support same-sex marriage  
The only president born outside the contiguous 48 states (Honolulu, Hawaii)

### Donald Trump (2017–2021)

*Historical Figures · American · [Notion](https://app.notion.com/p/23e44de2a65680778498da0800389883)*

![Donald Trump](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/16/Official_Presidential_Portrait_of_President_Donald_J._Trump_%282025%29.jpg/500px-Official_Presidential_Portrait_of_President_Donald_J._Trump_%282025%29.jpg)

US President (Republican)

Imposed travel sanctions on seven Muslim-majority countries, expanded Mexico-US border wall, enforced separation policy on the border. Rolled back environmental and business regulations, signed Tax Cuts and Jobs Act, and appointed 3 Supreme Court Justices

Withdrew the US from agreements on climate, trade, and Iran’s nuclear program. Initiated trade war with China

WRT COVID-19, downplayed severity, contradicted health officials, and signed the CARES Act (2.2 trillion stimulus bill, with one-time cash payments to individuals who submit a tax return, increased unemployment benefits, forgivable loans to small businesses, loans to corporations, and state and local governments).

Attempted to overturn the 2020 presidential election, culminating in the January 6 Capitol attack of 2021.

Impeached in 2019 for abuse of power and obstruction of Congress and in 2021 for incitement of insurrection; aquitted both times.

Found liable in civil cases for sexual abuse and defamation and for business fraud in 2023. Found guilty of falsifying business documents in 2024.

### Joe Biden (2021–2025)

*Historical Figures · American · [Notion](https://app.notion.com/p/23e44de2a65680cb9317d143a6a7da0c)*

![Joe Biden](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/68/Joe_Biden_presidential_portrait.jpg/500px-Joe_Biden_presidential_portrait.jpg)

US President (Democrat)

Opposed Gulf War in 1991 but voted in favor of the Iraq War Resolution in 2002

Signed American Rescue Plan Act (economic stimulus in response to COVID-19 and subsequent recession). Proposed Build Back Better Act; failed in Congress but partially incorporated into the Inflation Reduction Act (reduce deficit, promote clean energy, and lower prices of prescription drugs) of 2022.

Appointed Ketanji Brown Jackson to the Supreme Court of the US. Re-entered Paris agreement, oversaw complete withdrawal of US troops that ended war in Afghanistan (leading to the Taliban seizing control). Imposed sanctions on Russia following the Russian invasion of Ukraine.

During Gaza War, condemned actions of Hamas as terrorism, strongly supported Israel, and sent limited humanitarian aid to the Gaza Strip. Backed a temporary ceasefire proposal before his presidency ended.

Concerns about cognitive ability/dementia.

Fun facts:  
First president to serve with a female or African American vice president  
First president to turn 80 while in office.

### Donald Trump (2025)

*Historical Figures · American · [Notion](https://app.notion.com/p/23e44de2a656800881eff070d79102b9)*

![Donald Trump](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/16/Official_Presidential_Portrait_of_President_Donald_J._Trump_%282025%29.jpg/500px-Official_Presidential_Portrait_of_President_Donald_J._Trump_%282025%29.jpg)

US President (Democrat)

Started second term by initiating mass layoffs of federal workers.

Imposed tariffs on nearly all countries (including China, Canada, and Mexico) and signed One Big Beautiful Bill Act (which cut spending, particularly for Medicaid)

Focus on deportation of immigrants and extensive use of executive orders; challenged by over 300 lawsuits.
