### Shays’s Rebellion (1787)

*Historical Events · American · [Notion](https://app.notion.com/p/36a44de2a65680c58cb1d6d7acad968f)*

**Sources**

- [Khan Article on Shays’s Rebellion](https://www.khanacademy.org/humanities/us-history/road-to-revolution/creating-a-nation/a/shayss-rebellion)

<!-- page-notes:36a44de2a65680c58cb1d6d7acad968f -->
#### Page notes

- Context
  - Spurred by taxes levied by the state of Massachusetts
  - Led by Daniel Shays, who was taken to court for unpaid debts (which he couldn’t pay, since his labor during the Revolutionary War was unpaid and was wounded in battle); many others were in the same situation
  - All proposals for debt relief were rejected
- Progression
  - John Hancock initiated these policies (including rejecting debt relief proposals) and, sensing conflict, left position and was replaced by James Bowdoin
  - Bowdoin was much more confrontational, having civil actions collect unpaid taxes and raised taxes on top of it
  - Laborers and farmers led protests; Shattuck in 1782 led  a group to prevent tax collectors from collecting from rural workers, mobs seized confiscated property to return it to their owners
  - Militia was ordered to quell the protests, but the militia sympathized with the protestors; Bowdoin had to recruit a private militia
- Shays’s Rebellion (1787)
  - Shays led 1200 protestors to storm the federal armory in Springfield
  - Raised questions of what the government could do; technically didn’t have the powers to raise armies or regulate trade
  - Led Nationalists to proclaim the Articles of Confederation inadequate and meet for the Constitution; indicated that the government needed more power
