### Abraham Lincoln (1861–1865)

*Historical Figures · American · [Notion](https://app.notion.com/p/22444de2a65680a4a32de62a784f7235)*

![Abraham Lincoln](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/57/Abraham_Lincoln_1863_Portrait_%283x4_cropped%29.jpg/800px-Abraham_Lincoln_1863_Portrait_%283x4_cropped%29.jpg)

US President (Republican)

Rose in politics due to his anger following the Kansas-Nebraska Act. Southern States took his election as a threat and attacked Fort Sumter a month into his presidency. Devoted focus to the war, supervising the strategy and tactics, selecting the generals, implementing a naval blockade, suspending habeas corpus (which allows legal review of unlawful imprisonment) and taking other controversial actions.

Averted war with Britain by defusing the Trent Affair

Issued the Emancipation Proclamation and delivered the Gettysburg Address

Promoted the Thirteenth Amendment, which abolished slavery.

Worked to heal the US through Reconstruction

Fun facts  
Largely ranked the greatest president

#### Page notes

- Lincoln-Douglas Senatorial Race
  - *[Image in Notion](https://app.notion.com/p/22444de2a65680a4a32de62a784f7235)*
  - Lincoln largely unknown before the debates
  - Intense debates across Illinois
    - Douglas: Republicans posed a threat to the Constitution, were trying to destroy what the founding fathers created
    - Lincoln: Nation cannot survive if slave-free state division continued
      - House Divided speech
      - Believed the Dred-Scott ruling meant that Democrats were pushing to legalize slavery in all US
      - Purpose was not to outlaw slavery but to preserve the Union
        - Says that he believes that his race should be superior, but also that Black people should be entitled to the same rights that whites are
  - Douglas won the seat, but Lincoln propelled into the political spotlight
- Lincoln-Douglas-Breckenridge Presidential Race
  - Democrats split between Douglas and Breckenridge, giving Lincoln an advantage
    - To prevent Republican victory, border states organized the Constitution Party, nominating John Bell
      - Promised to end slavery agitation and preserve the union, but didn’t say how he’d do so
  - Lincoln only won 40% of the popular vote, but gained the majority of the electoral college

*[Image in Notion](https://app.notion.com/p/22444de2a65680a4a32de62a784f7235)*

- The Crittenden Compromise
  - Lincoln’s election prompted South States to leave the Union
  - Kentucky Senator John Crittenden proposed a compromise to keep Southern States from seceding before war broke out
  - Enshrine slavery in the Constitution and stipulate that it shall not be abolished; reestablish slavery south of the 36-30 line and make it illegal in the rest of the US (effectively reinstating the Missouri Compromise)
  - Failed to pass Congress and rejected by Lincoln
