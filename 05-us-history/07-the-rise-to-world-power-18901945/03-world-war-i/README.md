### Woodrow Wilson (1913–1921)

*Historical Figures · American · [Notion](https://app.notion.com/p/22a44de2a65680f0af63da1971dd7d0f)*

![Woodrow Wilson](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/b4/President_Woodrow_Wilson_in_1914%2C_Harris_%26_Ewing_%283x4_cropped_b%29.jpg/500px-President_Woodrow_Wilson_in_1914%2C_Harris_%26_Ewing_%283x4_cropped_b%29.jpg)

US President (Democratic)

Leading architect of the League of Nations (worldwide org dedicated to peace) in the Treaty of Versailles; foreign policy stance known as Wilsonianism, declared neutrality at the start of World War I

New Freedom domestic agenda, introduced the modern income tax via Revenue Act of 1913, Federal Reserve Act

Authorized imposed segregation in federal bureaucracy and opposed women’s suffrage

Criticized for supporting racial segregation, expanding the federal government

Credited for establishing modern liberalism and weakening power of large corporations

Fun facts  
The only Democrat to serve as president during the Progressive Era  
Intended to seek a third term before stroke in 1919
