### Theodore Roosevelt (1901–1909)

*Historical Figures · American · [Notion](https://app.notion.com/p/22944de2a65680079af9d65cef371ad1)*

![Theodore Roosevelt](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/7d/Theodore_Roosevelt_by_the_Pach_Bros_%284x5_cropped%29.jpg/500px-Theodore_Roosevelt_by_the_Pach_Bros_%284x5_cropped%29.jpg)

US President (Republican)

Leader of the progressive movement, fought for nature conservation, consumer protection, breaking trusts, and fairness for all citizens.

Began construction of the Panama canal, expanded the Navy, and sent the Great White Fleet to project naval power.

Ended the Russo-Japanese Wwar and won the 1906 Nobel Peace Prize

Fun fact:   
Youngest to become president  
First American to win a Nobel Prize  
Considered one of the greatest American presidents

### William Howard Taft (1909–1913)

*Historical Figures · American · [Notion](https://app.notion.com/p/22a44de2a6568091ba93e09374ee8c61)*

![William Howard Taft](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/a1/Cabinet_card_of_William_Howard_Taft_by_Pach_Brothers_-_Cropped_to_image.jpg/500px-Cabinet_card_of_William_Howard_Taft_by_Pach_Brothers_-_Cropped_to_image.jpg)

US President (Republican)

Focused on East Asia more than Europe

Often intervened to prop up or remove Latin American governments

Sought reductions to tariffs.

Fun facts  
Despite personal ambition to become chief justice, Taft declined repeated offers of appointment to the Supreme Court, believing his political work to be more important  
Hand-picked by Theodore Roosevelt, but leaned more towards the conservative wing rather than the progressive ring (which Roosevelt favored)  
First president and first Supreme Court justice to be interred at Arlington National Cemetery
