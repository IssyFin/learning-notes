### William McKinley (1897–1891)

*Historical Figures · American · [Notion](https://app.notion.com/p/22944de2a6568072b5d9c729c5130fe2)*

![William McKinley](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/6d/Mckinley.jpg/500px-Mckinley.jpg)

US President (Republican)

Led US in Spanish-American Aar, oversaw expansionism and annexed Hawaii, Puerto Rico, Guam, Philippines, and American Samoa

Proposed that high tariffs would restore prosperity, signed Dingley Tariff of 1897 to protect manufacturers from foreign competition, passed Gold Standard Act of 1900

Foreign policy emulated era’s overseas imperialism of the great powers in Oceania, Asia, and the Caribbean Sea. Declared war on Spain over granting independence to rebellious Cuba; US saw quick and decisive victory and US won Puerto Rico, Guam, and Philippines colonies while Cuba was promised independence. Philippines pushed for independence but US suppressed it
