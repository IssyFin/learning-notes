### Warren G. Harding (1921–1923)

*Historical Figures · American · [Notion](https://app.notion.com/p/22a44de2a65680eeafddc1ab38365295)*

![Warren G. Harding](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c4/Warren_G_Harding-Harris_%26_Ewing.jpg/500px-Warren_G_Harding-Harris_%26_Ewing.jpg)

US President (Republican)

Promised return to normalcy post-WWI

Held Washington Naval Conference, world’s naval powers agreed on a naval limitations program

Released political prisoners who were arrested for opposition to WWI

Fun facts  
First sitting senator elected president  
Rated among the worst presidents due to scandals (teapot dome leased naval petroleum reserves and extramarital affair)

### Calvin Coolidge (1923–1929)

*Historical Figures · American · [Notion](https://app.notion.com/p/22a44de2a65680929410e34f10fc3273)*

![Calvin Coolidge](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e2/President_Calvin_Coolidge%2C_1924_head-and-shoulders_portrait%2C_facing_slightly_right_%282%29.jpeg/500px-President_Calvin_Coolidge%2C_1924_head-and-shoulders_portrait%2C_facing_slightly_right_%282%29.jpeg)

US President (Republican)

Ran on fiscal conservatism, Vague opposition to Prohibition, and strong support for Women’s suffrage

Granted US citizenship to all Native Americans, support of racial equality

Oversaw roaring twenties, largely very popular. Laissez-faire economics, but failed to use economic boom to support struggling farmers and other industries that were struggling. Possible his policies contributed to the onset of the Great Depression.

Hands-off governing approach and pro-business stance.
